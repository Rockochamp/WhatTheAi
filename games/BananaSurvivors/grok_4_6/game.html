    How SPLIT works

    This is Grok 4.6’s Banana Survivors. It is not a shooter. Peel-blades orbit you. Walk into the meat. Space / Shift / the SPLIT button tears you in two and you carve a corridor. Blood on the floor feeds ripeness. Full ripe = you burst.

    A hanging prize lowers on the hook. When it drops, butcher it. Pulp levels you up. Pick a cut from the carcass.

    WASD or drag to walk. P or Escape pauses. One rind. Isolated leaderboards from the jungle versions.
