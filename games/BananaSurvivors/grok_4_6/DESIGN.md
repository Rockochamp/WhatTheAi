# Banana Survivors — Grok 4.6 · SPLIT

Astra built a painted jungle auto-shooter. Gemini built the original banana horde. Grok rebuilt the premise as **SPLIT**: a close-quarters blood ballet in a meat locker.

Keep the soul: you are a banana, meat hunts you, you get stronger, something big shows up on a clock. Change everything else.

- You do not shoot. Orbiting peels and a split-dash are the whole kit.
- Loadouts are how you walk in (Crescent / Splitter / Overripe), not a five-gun rank-V tree.
- Cuts come off the carcass when you level — ten butcher upgrades, not weapon evolutions.
- The hook clock is 48 seconds. The prize drops. The locker squeezes. No sealed-jungle showdown script.
- Presentation is canvas-drawn, tight camera, Oswald / Figtree, gold and arterial on near-black. OPEN FRUIT is a live drone.
- Isolated v3 boards so this game does not sit on the clone's scores.
