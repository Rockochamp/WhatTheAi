What is Banana Survivors?

Banana Survivors turns a banana into the hero of a horde-survival game. Keep moving, gather experience and choose upgrades while enemies close in. The three editions offer noticeably different combat: an illustrated jungle auto-shooter, a close-contact blade game, and the original German-language Gold Edition. The newer editions include blood and gore.

How to play

  1. Astra and Grok support WASD, arrow keys, mouse steering and touch controls.
  2. In Astra, weapons fire automatically. Use Space, Shift, right-click or the touch button to dash, then collect XP and choose upgrades.
  3. In Grok, the same action triggers Split. Orbiting peel-blades deal contact damage, so positioning is central to combat.

A few useful tips

In Astra, a 50-second hunt leads to a boss showdown. Read the attack warnings and keep an escape route open.

In Grok, kills and standing in blood build ripeness toward Burst. These rules differ from Astra, and the editions keep separate scoreboards.
