What is Banana Survivors?

Banana Survivors turns a banana into the hero of a horde-survival game. Keep moving, gather experience and choose upgrades while enemies close in. The four editions offer noticeably different combat: Claude Fable 5.2’s Peel Out is slapstick, with peels, sliding monkeys and chain-reaction pile-ups and no blood; GPT-6 Astra is an illustrated jungle auto-shooter; Grok 4.6 is a close-contact blade game; and Gemini 3 Pro is the original German-language Gold Edition. The Astra and Grok editions include blood and gore.

How to play

  1. All newer editions support WASD, arrow keys, mouse steering and touch controls. Claude also supports a gamepad.
  2. In Claude, peels are thrown automatically at the nearest monkey. Space, Shift, right-click, a second finger or the Dash button dashes: you are untouchable for a moment and bowl over anything in your way.
  3. In Astra, weapons fire automatically and the same action dashes. In Grok it triggers Split, and orbiting peel-blades deal contact damage.

A few useful tips

In Claude, thick crowds make long chains: five tumbles from one peel is a Domino, ten is a Strike. Monkeys that slide into a palm crash hard and shake coconuts down on the rest.

The king in Claude cannot be tipped. He charges in a straight line, so stand with a palm behind you and step aside.

In Astra, a 50-second hunt leads to a boss showdown. In Grok, kills and standing in blood build ripeness toward Burst. The editions keep separate scoreboards.
