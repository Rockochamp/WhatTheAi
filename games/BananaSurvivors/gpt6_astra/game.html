    THE SURVIVOR'S FIELD GUIDE

    Small fruit. Big plans.

    Move WASD, arrow keys, or hold and drag your mouse. On touchscreens, drag anywhere in the arena to create a joystick.

    Dash Space, Shift, right-click, or the DASH button. You are invulnerable during the dash. Aim with your movement; the last direction is remembered.

    Shoot Automatic. Your weapons target nearby enemies. Concentrate on movement and collecting the golden XP.

    Pause P or Escape. Select upgrades with 1 / 2 / 3; R rerolls. You can use every menu with the keyboard.

    Build something ridiculous

    Equip up to four weapon families. Improve each to rank V to unlock its evolution. Perks apply to your whole build. You start with two rerolls; each boss restores one.

    Read the jungle

    Amber lanes warn of charges. Orange circles mark incoming slams. Green orbs hurt. Golden diamonds grant XP; glowing supplies heal, attract XP, clear enemies, or trigger overripe frenzy. Follow the small gold arrow to a supply cache.

    One more wave

    Every 50 seconds, a champion enters. Defeat it for healing and an upgrade, then face the next wave. Kills build a streak multiplier and charge seven seconds of overripe firepower. Waves continue endlessly. Ranking score = combat score + 2 per second + 250 per defeated boss. All loadouts play the same rules.
