# Original art

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this game, then converted to WebP using Pillow. No third-party character assets or music samples. Runtime code crops the transparent atlas; the delivered artwork was not painted over programmatically.

## Character atlas — generation mode

Output: `characters.webp`, 1774 × 887, with alpha.

Generation brief: A production-ready eight-character sprite atlas in a four-column, two-row grid on transparent background. Premium hand-painted 3D cartoon indie game style; bold dark outlines, readable silhouettes, consistent light from upper left, full bodies with generous separation. A scrappy yellow curved banana hero with teal bandana, peel-flap coat, boots and brass seed blaster; an expressive meatball monster; a sausage hound; armored steak brute; purple spitter with lime throat; three-faced meat splitter; tiny one-eyed meatling; and a huge bone-crowned Cleaver King. Playful grotesque meat monsters, no gore, no text, no labels, no scenery. Three-quarter top-down view suitable for a jungle survivors game.

Source: `exec-88412016-e9ee-4fff-8299-acfa43827554.png` in the session's generated-images folder. The tight sprite rectangles are documented in `renderer.js`; a separate white silhouette cache supplies hit flashes.

## Jungle ground — generation mode

Output: `ground.webp`, 1254 × 1254.

Generation brief: Seamless top-down jungle floor tile for the same premium illustrated game. Dark teal moss, worn ancient stone fragments, subtle earth texture, occasional banana leaves. Roughly 80% visually quiet walkable surface and 20% restrained detail. Rich painterly material with soft moonlit illumination, no characters, walls, props, labels or text. Low contrast so golden XP, the banana hero and coral monsters remain legible in motion.

Source: `exec-3cbb2832-8df6-475f-9362-d12b6444582b.png`.

## Cover — reference-image generation mode

Output: `cover.webp`. Reference: the generated character atlas above.

Generation brief: Cinematic panoramic key art for Banana Survivors in the atlas's illustrated 3D cartoon style. The same teal-bandana banana hero dominates the right half, leaping with a brass seed blaster, golden seeds and sweeping banana arcs. Expressive meat monsters and the bone-crowned boss pour through an atmospheric moonlit jungle ruin behind him. Strong depth, warm golden rim light, teal shadows and a playful sense of impossible odds. Keep the left half darker and quieter for a title and menu. No text, logos or UI in the image.

Source: `exec-e8e08950-8860-4186-9419-b0c943baf35f.png`. The original PNGs remain in the local session’s generated-images folder; portable WebP assets live beside this document.

## Motion and sound

Character bobbing, tilt, squash, flashes, fading defeats, charge lanes, particles, orbital peels, coconut arcs and lightning are generated by game code. Blood spray, flesh and bone fragments, and floor stains are code-drawn in `gore.js`. The Meatgrinder soundtrack is original DSP synthesis rendered to two MP3 stems by `scripts/render-banana-audio.py`; combat Foley is synthesized and cached in `audio.js`. No third-party samples or recordings.

Final project assets: `veterans.webp` (1536 × 1024, alpha) and `cover-v3.webp` (1672 × 941). The original atlas, ground and original cover remain available. The new atlas replaces the hero and adds Carver, Bloat, Ribcannon, Broodmother and Marrow Witch crops in `renderer.js`. Generated images were converted to WebP with alpha preserved; character artwork was not painted over by code.

Built-in generation prompt: a strict three-column, two-row transparent atlas matching the original game's hand-painted style, with an old rotten banana veteran (dark rot patches, black stem, deep creases, exhausted eyes, eye scar, scowl, torn faded teal headband, patched peel coat, battered boots and brass blaster); a red sinewy meat mantis with ivory sickles; a swollen grey-green gas-filled meat sack; a skeletal rib-cage bone gunner; a purple spider-like broodmother with glowing eggs; and a floating bone-crowned marrow witch. Full bodies, clear gutters, consistent upper-left light, three-quarter top-down perspective, no text or scenery. A second built-in edit requested background extraction only, replacing the painted backdrop with true alpha while preserving all six characters and their arrangement.

Cover source: `exec-ff6b6858-e2f0-4484-936a-0e0c29ff6dc4.png`. Built-in edit prompt: use the original cover as the edit target and new atlas as identity reference; replace the young banana with the exact rotten veteran in the same firing pose and scale, add the Carver and Bloat among the small background enemies, preserve the moonlit ruins, composition, dark left half, golden weapon trails and original boss, and add blood-streaked stone and torn flesh for a grittier horror-comedy mood. No text, UI, logos or watermark.

The stronger gore remains animated Canvas artwork: arterial streaks, larger organ fragments, torn eye sockets with tissue strands, and broken ribs, within the original particle limits.
