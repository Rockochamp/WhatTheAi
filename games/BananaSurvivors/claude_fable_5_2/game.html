      How to play

      Nobody gets hurt. Much.

      You are a banana and the monkeys are hungry. You throw peels on your own. A monkey that takes a peel slides away from you and knocks into whoever is behind it, and they slide too.

      Make a pile-up

      Thick crowds make long chains. Five tumbles from one peel is a Domino, ten is a Strike. Monkeys that slide into a palm crash hard and shake coconuts down on their friends.

      Dash

      Dashing makes you untouchable for a moment and bowls over anything in your way. Use it to break out when you are surrounded.

      Who is chasing you

      Small ones are fast. Ones with a red band stay back and lob coconuts: step out of the red circle. Gorillas are heavy and flatten a whole row when they finally go over. Every two minutes the king arrives. He cannot be tipped, but he charges in a straight line, and palms do not move.

      Grow

      Green drops fill your level. Each level you pick one of three upgrades.
