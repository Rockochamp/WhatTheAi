      About Ken’s life

      Each turn is a few years. You are shown three things those years could go to and pick one. Now and then the times interrupt with a decision of their own.

      Five threads run through his life: health, joy, bonds, craft and means. Every choice pulls some up and lets others slip, and all of them fray a little each year if left alone. Bonds fray fastest.

      He can die of poor health at any age, and from sixty on of being old. The better his health, the longer the odds.

      At the end his life is scored: joy and bonds across the whole span, his craft at its best, the years he had, the things worth remembering, and how close he came to what he wanted. A short life counts for less.
