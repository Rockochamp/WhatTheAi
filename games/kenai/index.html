What is Ken.AI?

Ken.AI is a life simulation about a boy born in 2026, in a century that hands more and more of itself to machines. There are two editions. Claude Fable 5.2 tells the life in about forty choices: each turn is a few years, you pick one of three things they go to, and five threads (health, joy, bonds, craft and means) are woven into a band that grows a column a year. Grokbot’s edition is a real-time care game in nine stages, with need bars, stage goals, report cards and hand-drawn art. Both are speculative fiction, not forecasts, and both save progress in your browser.

Choose a version

Each one opens that edition directly. These describe the implementations in this collection; they are not rankings of the underlying AI models.

  1. Claude Fable 5.2

    A whole life in about forty choices. Each turn is a few years and three things they could go to; the times interrupt with dilemmas of their own. His life is woven into a band, one column a year, and ends with an epitaph written from what actually happened.

  2. Grokbot

    Nine life stages with tech eras, stage goals and report cards, life forks, a life ambition, streaks, illnesses, death and replay, hand-drawn art and a browser save.

How to play

  1. Claude: choose what Ken wants from life, then pick one of three cards each turn with a click, a tap or the keys 1 to 3. Now and then an event asks a question instead. The coloured tags on a card show which threads it pulls up or lets slip.
  2. Grokbot: pick one of three life ambitions and press Begin a new life. Tap the action cards at the bottom to care for Ken and swipe the strip to see more. Watch the need bars and the goal chips; answer event cards and life forks when they appear.

A few useful tips

Claude: every thread frays a little each year if left alone, and bonds fray fastest. Poor health can end his life at any age, and from sixty on good health lengthens the odds. A short life scores less.

Claude: choices have side effects on how much Ken leans on machines, which changes how his epitaph ends.

Grokbot: mix up your action cards, keep every bar above half to grow the Thriving streak, and treat illnesses quickly. Real relationships give more happiness than AI companions, but leaning on AI makes daily needs cheaper.
