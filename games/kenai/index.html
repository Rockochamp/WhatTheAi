What is Ken.AI?

Ken.AI is a life simulation in nine stages, from baby to elder. Before Ken is born you pick a life ambition, such as Family first, Career legend or Analog soul. Ken is born in 2026 and each stage is a technology era, from AI baby monitors and talking toys to agent teams, longevity therapy and a digital legacy. Every stage has goals and ends with a report card, big life forks decide where he goes next, and event cards show what each choice changed. When he dies, an epitaph sums up his life, his ambition, his grades and his life score. Progress is saved in your browser.

How to play

  1. Pick one of three life ambitions on the title screen, then press Begin a new life. Tap the action cards at the bottom to care for Ken and swipe the strip to see more.
  2. Watch the need bars and the 🎯 goal chips under the top bar. Low bars hurt health, and health at zero ends the life.
  3. Answer event cards and life forks when they appear; the card then shows what changed. Use the speed button to run time faster and the menu button to return to the title screen.

A few useful tips

Mix up your action cards: repeating the same card works less well, while variety builds a combo with bonus stars.

Keep every bar above half to grow the 🔥 Thriving streak, and finish stage goals for a good report card. Good grades carry into the next stage.

Real relationships give more happiness than AI companions, but leaning on AI makes daily needs cheaper. Treat illnesses quickly and keep some savings for old age.
