Leo’s Fire Evolution

Help a lion cub with a small flame relight ten dark places and grow his fire through ten forms.

What is Leo’s Fire Evolution?

Leo is a lion cub with a flame for a mane, and his fire changes form ten times, from the First Spark to the Eternal Flame. There are two editions and neither has enemies or time limits. Claude Fable 5.2 makes it a walk in the dark: you steer Leo freely through ten places where his own flame is the only light, gather fireflies, find and light the sleeping lanterns, and each new form adds an ability such as gliding over water or a roar that reaches farther. GPT 6.2 Astra’s edition is a pixel-art trail game on a board of tiles, with sparks to collect, relics to wake and hidden moon moths.

How to play

  1. Claude: hold a finger or the mouse where Leo should go, or tap once to send him there. The arrow keys and WASD work too. Press the Roar button or Space to light lanterns, call fireflies and wake animals inside the ring.
  2. Claude: light every lantern to open the gate, then walk through it. His flame leans toward the nearest lantern that is still dark.
  3. Astra: tap or click the ground to send Leo along a path, or use WASD or the arrow keys for single steps. Collect three golden sparks, light both relics with Space or Cast fire, then walk into the open stone gate.

A few useful tips

Claude: a lantern is worth 100 points plus 20 for every firefly with Leo when it lights, so gather fireflies first. Wind sends half of them home; a roar stills the wind for a while. Each place also hides two sleeping animals worth 250 each.

Claude: the journey is saved after every place. Stop at any point to post your total under a name picked from two lists, or light all ten places for the Eternal Flame.

Astra: each world has three optional moon moths. A cleared world earns 500 points, plus 150 per moth and one point for every step below 100. The ember ledger keeps your ten best trails per world, and Post score sends one to that world’s shared leaderboard.
