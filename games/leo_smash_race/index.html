Leos Smash Racer

Race, boost and smash with Leo’s toys in three different takes on his racing game.

Play the Claude Fable 5.2 editionCompare all versions

What is Leos Smash Racer?

Leo designed the racing game at age 4. Claude Fable 5.2 built it for a four-year-old to actually play: a road that runs into the screen, block towers, cones and rubber ducks to drive straight through, two big steering buttons and a race that cannot be lost. Grokbot’s side-scrolling edition lets you pick a hero, change lanes, fly and chain smashes. GPT-6.1 Sol turns ten of Leo’s real toys into racers for four top-down circuits, with free steering, drifting, rival cars and vehicle damage. Each edition has its own cube warm-up and leaderboard.

Choose a version

Each one opens that edition directly. These describe the implementations in this collection; they are not rankings of the underlying AI models.

  1. Claude Fable 5.2

    A road that runs into the screen, six toy cars and three roads full of block towers, cones and rubber ducks to drive through. Nothing can go wrong, a helper steers a little for small hands, and a colour cube solved in three taps fills the boost. In English and German.

  2. GPT-6.1 Sol

    Top-down circuit racing with ten toy racers including Knuckles, drifting, vehicle damage, nitro and a breakbeat soundtrack that intensifies on the final lap.

  3. Grokbot

    Side-scrolling action with ten heroes, four tracks, lane changes, flight, assist mode, 2×2 or 3×3 cube warm-up and an optional world leaderboard.

How to play

  1. Claude: pick a car and a road. Steer with the two big buttons, a finger on the left or right half of the screen, or the arrow keys. Press Boost (or Space) when the button turns red, and H or the blue button to honk. Solve the colour cube first by tapping its sides, or skip it.
  2. Grokbot: Pick a hero and track. Solve or skip the cube warm-up, then use the arrow keys or swipe to change lanes. Hold Space or the FLY button to climb and tap BOOST when your special is charged.
  3. GPT-6.1 Sol: Pick a toy, track and class. Steer with A/D or the arrows, use Down to drift, Shift for nitro, Space to jump and E for your special. Touch controls are available on touchscreens.

A few useful tips

Claude: every toy you hit fills the boost a little, and hits in quick succession build a chain up to ×5. Points count double while the boost burns, so save it for a stretch with a lot to smash.

Claude: with the helper on the car drifts toward the next toy by itself; turn it off for sharper bends and faster rivals. Racing names come from two lists, so nobody types anything.

Grokbot: Assist mode steers toward smashable targets. A solved cube gives you a full boost, and quick successive smashes build a combo up to ×5.

GPT-6.1 Sol: Crew offers steering assistance and lighter damage. Use ramps to reach bonus rings, chain up to six smashes, and submit finished races under a preset alias to the leaderboard for your circuit and class.
