What is Leos Smash Racer?

Leo designed the racing game at age 4. Grokbot’s side-scrolling edition lets you pick a hero, change lanes, fly and chain smashes. GPT-6.1 Sol turns nine of Leo’s real toys into racers for four top-down circuits, with free steering, drifting, rival cars and vehicle damage. Each edition has its own special moves, cube warm-up and world leaderboard.

How to play

  1. Grokbot: Pick a hero and track. Solve or skip the cube warm-up, then use the arrow keys or swipe to change lanes. Hold Space or the FLY button to climb and tap BOOST when your special is charged.
  2. GPT-6.1 Sol: Pick a toy, track and class. Steer with A/D or the arrows, use Down to drift, Shift for nitro, Space to jump and E for your special. Touch controls are available on touchscreens.

A few useful tips

Grokbot: Assist mode steers toward smashable targets. A solved cube gives you a full boost, and quick successive smashes build a combo up to ×5.

GPT-6.1 Sol: Crew offers steering assistance and lighter damage. Use ramps to reach bonus rings, chain up to six smashes, and submit finished races under a preset alias to the leaderboard for your circuit and class.
