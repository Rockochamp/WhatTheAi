What is Smarleos Smash Racer?

Race two laps against four rivals across four circuits. The garage includes a dinosaur monster truck, a twin-propeller aircraft, a toy sword and six other toys, each with its own special move and illustrated bitmap sprite.

How to play

  1. Pick a toy, circuit and class in the garage. Crew has steering assistance and lighter damage. Pro has faster rivals.
  2. Use A/D or the arrow keys to steer, S or Down to drift, Shift for nitro, Space to jump, F to fire and E for your special. Touch buttons appear on supported devices.
  3. Land on boost pads, smash crates, and grab shields and repairs. Jump or use ramps to reach bonus rings.

A few useful tips

Smashes build combos up to ×6. Your result also includes race position, finish time and vehicle condition. Each pit stop costs 500 points.

An optional 2×2 or 3×3 cube fills the Crew nitro tank if solved. Submit finished races with a preset alias to each circuit’s Crew or Pro leaderboard.
