What is Cosmic Dodge?

Cosmic Dodge is a free browser arcade game about keeping your ship alive in a falling asteroid field. The five versions share that starting idea, but add different hazards, tools and scoring rules. The Claude Fable 5.2 edition rewards flying close: near misses build Flow, Flow multiplies your score, and you can spend it to slow time. Try it first, then see how each model changes the experience.

Choose a version

Each one opens that edition directly. These describe the implementations in this collection; they are not rankings of the underlying AI models.

  1. Claude Fable 5.2

    Graze hazards to build Flow, then spend it to slow time. Six sectors with walls, spinners, ice streams, comets and crossfire, a shared daily run, gamepad support and a soundtrack that grows as you climb.

  2. GPT-6 Astra

    One life, shields, a rechargeable Burst, warned comets and level-based rankings. Includes the Terminal Velocity soundtrack.

  3. Grok 4.6

    Adds gravity wells, slingshots, cluster rocks and solar shears. Close calls refund Burst recharge time.

  4. Gemini 3.5 Flash (High)

    A shooting-and-dodging edition with comets, giant meteors, weapon pickups and shields.

  5. ChatGPT o3-mini-high

    The simpler asteroid-dodging challenge: one collision ends a run and every ten dodges advances the level.

How to play

  1. In the Claude edition, steer with WASD, the arrow keys, the mouse, a finger or a gamepad. Hold Space, Shift, the left mouse button or a second finger to warp time. P or Escape pauses.
  2. In the Astra and Grok editions, move with WASD or arrow keys, or drag with a mouse or finger. Press Space or the Burst button for brief protection.
  3. Ten cleared asteroids advance a level in the Claude, Astra and Grok editions. Claude ranks by points; Astra and Grok rank by level.

A few useful tips

In the Claude edition, a graze is a pass within a hair of a hazard. Grazes fill Flow up to an ×8 multiplier, and warping burns Flow, so every escape costs some score.

Today’s run in the Claude edition uses one seed for every player and has its own ranking, which resets at midnight UTC.

Each edition keeps separate records. Different mechanics mean a score in one version is not an equivalent result in another.

Free to try in your browser

No installation or account is needed. The interactive experience needs JavaScript. Browser storage and network access may be used for preferences, records or shared features. Read about the project and its data use.
