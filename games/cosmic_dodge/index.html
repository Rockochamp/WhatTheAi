What is Cosmic Dodge?

Cosmic Dodge is a free browser arcade game about keeping your ship alive in a falling asteroid field. The four versions share that starting idea, but add different hazards, weapons and scoring rules. Try the direct survival challenge first, then see how each model changes the experience.

How to play

  1. In the Astra and Grok editions, move with WASD or arrow keys, or drag with a mouse or finger.
  2. Press Space or the Burst button for brief protection. P or Escape pauses the run.
  3. Ten cleared asteroids advance a level in Astra and Grok. Style points are tracked separately from level progression.

A few useful tips

Leave room to escape instead of hugging a corner. Save Burst for crowded passages.

Astra and Grok have separate records. Different mechanics mean a score in one version is not an equivalent result in another.
