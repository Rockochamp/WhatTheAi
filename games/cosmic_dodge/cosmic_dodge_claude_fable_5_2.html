    How to play

    Dodge

    Everything with a warm glow ends your run. You carry one shield; a mint hexagon gives you another when it is gone. Every ten rocks you outlast takes you up a level, and every fifth level opens a new sector with a new kind of trouble.

    Graze

    Passing within a hair of a hazard is a graze. Grazes fill your Flow, and Flow multiplies every point you earn, up to ×8. Stop grazing and it slowly drains back to a small reserve.

    Warp

    Hold Space, Shift, the left mouse button, a second finger or a gamepad button to slow the world to a third of its speed. Warping burns Flow, so every escape costs you some of your multiplier.

    Two kinds of run

    Endless gives you a fresh field each time. Today’s run uses one seed for every player and has its own ranking, which starts again at midnight UTC.
