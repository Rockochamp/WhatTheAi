    How it works

    Set a beat

    Your first two taps set the tempo: anything from 60 to 200 beats a minute. Every tap after that has to land within 50 milliseconds of the beat. Each tap you keep is a point.

    Read the ring and the tape

    The ring closes on the drum exactly on the beat. The tape at the bottom draws every tap: above the line you were early, below it late.

    The acts

    After eight easy beats the acts begin, eight beats each, with four beats of rest between them. The click leaves. The lights go out. A second drummer arrives at the wrong tempo. The ring starts lying. After seven acts they come back two at a time.

    Spare misses

    Eight dead-on hits in a row (within 16 milliseconds) earn one forgiven miss. You can hold two.

    With reduced motion, the room stays still and nothing flashes; the sound still tries to fool you.
