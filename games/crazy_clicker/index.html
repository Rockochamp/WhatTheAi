What is Crazy Clicker?

Crazy Clicker is a timing game rather than an idle clicker. Your first two taps establish a rhythm; keep matching that interval to build a streak. The challenge is consistency, not clicking as fast as possible. All three editions use a ±50 millisecond window. The Claude Fable 5.2 edition turns the streak into a show: every eight beats a new act tries to pull you off your tempo, and at the end you see every tap drawn against the beat.

Choose a version

Each one opens that edition directly. These describe the implementations in this collection; they are not rankings of the underlying AI models.

  1. Claude Fable 5.2

    A show in acts: after eight easy beats the click leaves, the lights go out, a second drummer plays the wrong tempo and the ring starts lying. A live tape draws every tap, and the result shows where you rushed or dragged.

  2. Grok 4.6

    Visible beat rings, a ±16 millisecond perfect window, the Ember save and a reactive soundtrack.

  3. Gemini 2.5 Pro

    The original two-tap rhythm challenge, focused on repeating your chosen interval and extending a streak.

How to play

  1. Tap, click or press Space. Two taps lock the beat. The Claude edition accepts 60 to 200 beats a minute; the Grok edition needs at least 70 milliseconds between the two taps.
  2. Stay within the timing window to keep the streak alive. In the Claude and Grok editions the ring closes on the drum exactly on the beat.
  3. In the Claude edition Escape leaves a run; in the Grok edition P or Escape pauses. Check each edition’s on-screen instructions before starting.

A few useful tips

Choose a comfortable pace you can repeat. In the Claude edition, count out loud when the lights go out and ignore the second drummer.

Claude gives one forgiven miss for eight hits in a row within 16 milliseconds, up to two. Grok rewards five consecutive perfect hits with Ember, a one-hit save.

Leaderboards are separate between editions.
