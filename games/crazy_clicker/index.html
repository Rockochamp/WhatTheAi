What is Crazy Clicker?

Crazy Clicker is a timing game rather than an idle clicker. Your first two taps establish a rhythm; keep matching that interval to build a streak. The challenge is consistency, not clicking as fast as possible. Both editions run in the browser and offer a different way to feel the beat.

How to play

  1. In the Grok edition, tap, click or press Space. Two taps lock the beat, with a minimum interval of 70 milliseconds.
  2. Stay within the timing window to keep the streak alive. Grok uses a ±50 millisecond hit window and a tighter perfect window.
  3. P or Escape pauses Grok. Check each edition’s on-screen instructions before starting.

A few useful tips

Choose a comfortable pace you can repeat. Watch the collapsing rings in Grok to anticipate the next beat.

Grok rewards five consecutive perfect hits with Ember, a one-hit save. Leaderboards are separate between editions.
