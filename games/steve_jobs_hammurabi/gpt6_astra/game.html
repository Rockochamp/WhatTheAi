THE RULER'S HANDBOOK

Build a lasting kingdom.

Survive ten years. Finish with as many people as possible. Your people are the “pawns” of the original game, and your final population is your score. Your reign ends early if no one remains.

01 · Trade before you spend

Land costs 18–28 bushels per acre, changing each year. Buy or sell, never both. Selling releases food immediately; buying increases next year's potential.

02 · Seeds become a harvest

Each planted acre costs 1 bushel and yields 3–7. One person can tend 10 acres. You must own the land and pay for seeds and feeding before the harvest arrives.

03 · Hunger has a memory

20 bushels per person restores health. Feeding less builds hunger over several years; sustained hunger causes deaths. Good feeding attracts immigrants. Very low rations can cause emigration.

04 · Expect the unexpected

Rats can eat 5–20% of stored food; favorable weather can add 5–15% to the harvest; plague can take 5–20% of your people. Keep a reserve. Luck helps, but careful planning matters.
