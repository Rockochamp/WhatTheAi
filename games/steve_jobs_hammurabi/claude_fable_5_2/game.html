    Chronicle

      Made with Claude Fable 5.2. The other editions are in the guide.

      Rankings

        Rules

        Each year

        Trade land at the year’s price, sow up to ten acres a person, and set how many bushels each person eats. Seed costs one bushel an acre. The harvest returns three to seven bushels for each acre sown.

        Hunger

        Twenty bushels a person holds hunger steady and slowly heals it. Less than twenty makes hunger build. Past the breaking point people starve, so a lean year or two is survivable and a third is not.

        Growth

        The better the rations, the likelier newcomers arrive; at thirty bushels it is certain. A bigger city with more land draws more of them, up to fifty a year. Rats, plague and a generous river come when they come.

        Fate and the scribe

        A reign’s luck is fixed when it begins. Today’s fate is the same for every player and has its own ranking, so you can try it again with what you learned. The scribe will advise you three times in a reign. At the end you see what the scribe would have made of the same luck.

        Score

        The people left after ten years. A reign ends early if nobody is left.
