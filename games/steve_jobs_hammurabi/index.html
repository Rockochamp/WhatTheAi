What is Steve Jobs – Hammurabi?

Steve Jobs – Hammurabi is a free turn-based resource management game. Each year, choose how much land to buy or sell, how many acres to plant, and how well to feed your population. Your aim is to finish a ten-year reign with as many people as possible while surviving shortages and random events.

How to play

  1. Enter a ruler name if you want, then start a reign.
  2. Set annual land, planting and feeding decisions. Buying and selling land cannot happen in the same year.
  3. Confirm the year and read the report before making the next decisions. A reign ends after ten years or when population reaches zero.

A few useful tips

One person can farm ten acres; planting also costs food for seed. Keep enough food to feed people before expanding.

Harvests and events vary. The Astra budget and feeding-risk preview help you check decisions, but cannot guarantee the next harvest.
