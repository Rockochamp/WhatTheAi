What is Yes! I Said It.?

Yes! I Said It. is an anonymous opinion board. Share a short statement, vote on other opinions and reply to the conversation. GPT-6.1 Sol Medium is the default edition, and Gemini 2.5 Pro remains available. Both versions connect to the same public discussion board.

How to use it

  1. Write an opinion, then use the publish button. Sol accepts up to 420 characters and keeps a local draft.
  2. Open a statement to read and add replies. Agree or disagree using the voting controls.
  3. In Sol, switch between Latest, Loved, Hated and Divisive; load more posts or search the statements already loaded.

Before you begin

Posts are public. Avoid personal information, threats, hate and spam. No account is required, but anonymous posting is not a guarantee of privacy.

Sol can hide posts on your device and restore them later. This does not remove them from the shared board. An internet connection is needed for the live discussion.
