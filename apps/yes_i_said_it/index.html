What is Yes! I Said It.?

Yes! I Said It. is an anonymous opinion board. Share a short statement, vote on other opinions and reply to the conversation. All three editions read and write the same public board. The Claude Fable 5.2 edition shows statements one at a time and reveals the split only after you answer, so the room cannot lead you; the GPT-6.1 Sol Medium and Gemini 2.5 Pro editions present the board as a feed.

Choose a version

Each one opens that edition directly. These describe the implementations in this collection; they are not rankings of the underlying AI models.

  1. Claude Fable 5.2

    Statements arrive one at a time on a sign. Answer yes or no by button, key or swipe, and only then see how the room split. A wall view, threads, a private hide list, and a cover over strong language until you ask to see it.

  2. GPT-6.1 Sol Medium

    A responsive opinion board with conversation starters, saved drafts, loaded-post search, thread links and optional music.

  3. Gemini 2.5 Pro

    The existing opinion-board interface, kept as an alternative for posting, voting and reading the same public conversations.

How to use it

  1. In the Claude edition, answer the sign with the Yes! or No. paddle, the arrow keys or a swipe; Pass skips a statement. Switch to The wall to browse everything loaded, sorted by latest, most yes, most no or most split.
  2. Write an opinion with Say something (Claude) or the composer (Sol, Gemini). Statements can be up to 420 characters.
  3. Open a statement to read and add replies. Pressing the same answer again takes your vote back.

Before you begin

Posts are public. Avoid personal information, threats, hate and spam. No account is required, but anonymous posting is not a guarantee of privacy.

Hiding a statement only affects your own browser in the Claude and Sol editions; it stays on the shared board. The Claude edition also covers statements with strong language until you ask to see them.

An internet connection is needed for the live discussion. Votes are remembered per browser, so they are casual signals rather than a verified poll.

Free to try in your browser

No installation or account is needed. The interactive board needs JavaScript and an internet connection. Browser storage and network access may be used for preferences, records or shared features. Read about the project and its data use.
