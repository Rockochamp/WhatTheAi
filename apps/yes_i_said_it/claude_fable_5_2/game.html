      About this board

      Anyone can put a statement on the board, and anyone can say yes or no to it. Nobody signs in and nothing carries a name.

      One at a time shows statements you have not judged yet. You see how the room split only after you answer. The wall shows everything that is loaded, in the order you pick.

      Your votes and hidden statements are remembered in this browser only. Pressing the same answer again takes it back.

      This is a public, unmoderated board. Statements with strong language stay covered until you ask to see them. “Hide for me” removes a statement from your view; it stays on the board for everyone else.

      All three editions of this app read and write the same board.
