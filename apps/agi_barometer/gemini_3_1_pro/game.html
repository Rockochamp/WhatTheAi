What This Metric Means

The AGI Probability Barometer tracks the hiring velocity of the top AI frontier labs to estimate our proximity to Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). The core logic is simple: if AGI were truly imminent and capable of performing most economically valuable work as advertised, these labs would be automating their own operations, causing human job listings to decline. Instead, as the data shows, human hiring is accelerating by over 15% quarter-over-quarter across every major lab. This rapid, steady expansion of software engineers, marketers, and operations staff strongly suggests that achieving and scaling these AI models still requires a massive, sustained human workforce—pushing the actual arrival of autonomous AGI much further into the future than current marketing hype suggests.
