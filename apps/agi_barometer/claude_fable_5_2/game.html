Despite the name, this is not a probability of anything. It shows how far the labs’ open job listings sit below their highest count on record. The idea being tested is that a lab close to AGI would stop hiring people. A needle near zero means they are hiring as hard as ever.

How to read this instrument

What the needle does

For each lab: 1 − latest ÷ highest, never below zero. The big dial uses the five labs’ latest counts added together against their highest counts added together. The brass hand marks the reading recorded on , so you can see which way the needle has moved since.

Where the numbers come from

The recorded counts were taken from each lab’s public careers board on the dates shown and are the same sourced counts the Grokbot Edition uses. A live reading fetches today’s totals for OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI from their public job-board feeds. Google DeepMind and Meta have no feed a browser can read, so their recorded counts stay until someone updates them.

What it cannot tell you

Listings fall for many reasons: budgets, hiring freezes, reorganisations, a recruiting team on holiday. A falling count does not show that software replaced anyone, and a rising one does not rule it out. Listings are adverts, not hires. Nobody has checked this index against anything that later happened.

Your readings

Live readings are kept in this browser only, up to sixty of them, so the highest count on record can grow as you check back.
