The Obsolescence Logic

The barometer operates on a clean premise: if AGI were imminent and capable of automating human-level tasks, AI companies would automate their own software engineering, operations, and marketing listings.

A declining trend in job listings (Q3 < Peak) increases AGI probability. Conversely, continuous expansion of the human workforce (Q3 ≥ Peak) pushes the estimated arrival of autonomous AGI further back.

P(AGI) = max( 0%, 100% * ( 1 - Q3 / max(Q1, Q2, Q3) ) )
