Nine titles, 30 versions, four model families. Nothing to download and no account.

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. Every game and app opens in your browser, on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to install and no account to make.

What does “built by an AI model” mean here?

The code of each edition, and usually its artwork and sound, was generated by the model named on it. Most started from a single prompt; some were refined with follow-up requests. The labels describe the editions in this collection. They are not benchmark scores.

Which version should I try first?

The first one listed under each title is the newest. Each guide, such as the one for Cosmic Dodge, explains the controls and what the other versions do differently.

Are scores and posts shared?

Some games have a shared leaderboard and the opinion board is public, so a nickname, score or post can be seen by other visitors. Everything else stays in your browser. Read about data use.

Can I read the code?

All of it is public in the GitHub repository, one folder per edition.
