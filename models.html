Claude editions

Nine editions in this collection carry a Claude label (Anthropic): Claude Fable 5.2.

  1. Cosmic Dodge

    Claude Fable 5.2

    Graze hazards to build Flow, then spend it to slow time. Six sectors with walls, spinners, ice streams, comets and crossfire, a shared daily run, gamepad support and a soundtrack that grows as you climb.

  2. Steve Jobs – Hammurabi

    Claude Fable 5.2

    The kingdom as a living mosaic, sliders with a live grain ledger, and a scribe who advises three times. Luck is fixed per reign, so you see how the scribe would have ruled the same fate, and today’s fate is shared by every player.

  3. Crazy Clicker

    Claude Fable 5.2

    A show in acts: after eight easy beats the click leaves, the lights go out, a second drummer plays the wrong tempo and the ring starts lying. A live tape draws every tap, and the result shows where you rushed or dragged.

  4. Yes! I Said It.

    Claude Fable 5.2

    Statements arrive one at a time on a sign. Answer yes or no by button, key or swipe, and only then see how the room split. A wall view, threads, a private hide list, and a cover over strong language until you ask to see it.

  5. Banana Survivors

    Claude Fable 5.2

    Peel Out: nobody gets shot. You throw peels, monkeys slip, and each slipping monkey bowls over the ones behind it. Pile-ups, palm crashes that rain coconuts, a dash that bowls through the crowd, fourteen upgrades and a king every two minutes. No blood.

  6. AGI Probability Barometer

    Claude Fable 5.2

    A brass barometer you can tap. The needle shows how far five labs’ job listings sit below their peak; tapping the glass takes a live reading from three labs’ public job boards, and a second hand marks the recorded reading for comparison.

  7. Ken.AI

    Claude Fable 5.2

    A whole life in about forty choices. Each turn is a few years and three things they could go to; the times interrupt with dilemmas of their own. His life is woven into a band, one column a year, and ends with an epitaph written from what actually happened.

  8. Leos Smash Racer

    Claude Fable 5.2

    A road that runs into the screen, six toy cars and three roads full of block towers, cones and rubber ducks to drive through. Nothing can go wrong, a helper steers a little for small hands, and a colour cube solved in three taps fills the boost. In English and German.

  9. Leo’s Fire Evolution

    Claude Fable 5.2

    A walk through ten places in the dark. Leo’s flame is the only light until he finds the lanterns, and each one he lights adds a note to the place’s tune. Fireflies follow him, wind blows them home, a roar lights what is near, and his fire takes a new form with a new ability in every place. In English and German.

GPT editions

Eight editions in this collection carry a GPT label (OpenAI): ChatGPT o3-mini-high, GPT 6.2 Astra, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol Medium.

  1. Cosmic Dodge

    GPT-6 Astra

    One life, shields, a rechargeable Burst, warned comets and level-based rankings. Includes the Terminal Velocity soundtrack.

  2. Cosmic Dodge

    ChatGPT o3-mini-high

    The simpler asteroid-dodging challenge: one collision ends a run and every ten dodges advances the level.

  3. Steve Jobs – Hammurabi

    GPT-6 Astra

    A responsive budget, annual reports, chronicle and a living 3D kingdom. A panorama fallback keeps the strategy interface available without WebGL.

  4. Yes! I Said It.

    GPT-6.1 Sol Medium

    A responsive opinion board with conversation starters, saved drafts, loaded-post search, thread links and optional music.

  5. Banana Survivors

    GPT-6 Astra

    An illustrated jungle, automatic weapons, evolutions, stage bosses, a dash and a short death replay.

  6. Leos Smash Racer

    GPT 6.2 Astra

    Ricochet Rally turns the racetrack into a pinball table. Race as Leo’s Crystal Golem, Dino Rex or Knuckles on three tables. Bank off rails, open vaults and chase your fastest ghost.

  7. Leos Smash Racer

    GPT-6.1 Sol

    Top-down circuit racing with ten toy racers including Knuckles, drifting, vehicle damage, nitro and a breakbeat soundtrack that intensifies on the final lap.

  8. Leo’s Fire Evolution

    GPT 6.2 Astra

    A pixel-art exploration game with ten fire forms, tap-to-walk paths, hidden moon moths and a separate leaderboard for each world.

Gemini editions

Six editions in this collection carry a Gemini label (Google): Gemini 2.5 Pro, Gemini 3 Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro (High), Gemini 3.5 Flash (High).

  1. Cosmic Dodge

    Gemini 3.5 Flash (High)

    A shooting-and-dodging edition with comets, giant meteors, weapon pickups and shields.

  2. Crazy Clicker

    Gemini 2.5 Pro

    The original two-tap rhythm challenge, focused on repeating your chosen interval and extending a streak.

  3. Yes! I Said It.

    Gemini 2.5 Pro

    The existing opinion-board interface, kept as an alternative for posting, voting and reading the same public conversations.

  4. Banana Survivors

    Gemini 3 Pro

    The German-language Gold Edition: a voxel banana, automatic combat, XP upgrades and endless boss waves.

  5. AGI Probability Barometer

    Gemini 3.5 Flash (High)

    Three stored quarterly inputs per lab, a combined indicator, trend chart and interactive formula details.

  6. AGI Probability Barometer

    Gemini 3.1 Pro (High)

    The earlier two-quarter dashboard, with one gauge per lab and a simpler decline-based formula.

Grok editions

Seven editions in this collection carry a Grok label (xAI): Grok 3 Reasoning, Grok 4.6, Grokbot, Grokbot Edition.

  1. Cosmic Dodge

    Grok 4.6

    Adds gravity wells, slingshots, cluster rocks and solar shears. Close calls refund Burst recharge time.

  2. Steve Jobs – Hammurabi

    Grok 3 Reasoning

    The original form-based strategy version, with annual decisions, resource totals, help and local/global rankings.

  3. Crazy Clicker

    Grok 4.6

    Visible beat rings, a ±16 millisecond perfect window, the Ember save and a reactive soundtrack.

  4. Banana Survivors

    Grok 4.6

    SPLIT puts you in a meat locker with orbiting peel-blades, a splitting dash, ripeness and hanging boss prizes.

  5. AGI Probability Barometer

    Grokbot Edition

    Live job-listing counts with source links and dates, one hiring-drop index formula, a dashed live-count segment on the chart and a no-data state for labs without a verified count.

  6. Ken.AI

    Grokbot

    Nine life stages with tech eras, stage goals and report cards, life forks, a life ambition, streaks, illnesses, death and replay, hand-drawn art and a browser save.

  7. Leos Smash Racer

    Grokbot

    Side-scrolling action with ten heroes, four tracks, lane changes, flight, assist mode, 2×2 or 3×3 cube warm-up and an optional world leaderboard.
